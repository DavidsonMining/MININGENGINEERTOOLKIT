Common Mining Formulas

Stripping Ratio

SR = Overburden / Ore

Used to compare the amount of waste material to ore.

Density

Density = Mass / Volume

Used to determine the mass per unit volume of a material.

Porosity

Porosity = (Pore Volume / Total Volume) × 100

Expresses the percentage of pore space in a material.

Tonnage

Tonnage = Volume × Density

Used to estimate the mass of material from its volume and density.

Recovery

Recovery = (Recovered Metal / Available Metal) × 100

Expresses the percentage of valuable metal recovered.
