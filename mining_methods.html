Introduction

Mining methods are the techniques used to extract valuable minerals from the Earth's crust. The method selected depends on factors such as the depth, shape, size, grade and geological conditions of the deposit.

Surface Mining

🏔️ Open-Pit Mining

A surface mining method in which ore is extracted from a large open excavation. It is commonly used for deposits located relatively close to the surface.

🚜 Strip Mining

A surface mining method where overburden is removed in strips to expose and extract relatively shallow, often tabular mineral deposits.

Underground Mining

⛏️ Room and Pillar

Ore is extracted from rooms while pillars of material are left in place to support the roof.

🕳️ Sublevel Stoping

An underground method in which ore is extracted from stopes developed between sublevels. It is generally suited to relatively steep and competent ore bodies.

Key Factors in Method Selection

Important factors include deposit depth, ore-body geometry, rock strength, ore grade, stripping ratio, production requirements, safety and economic conditions.
