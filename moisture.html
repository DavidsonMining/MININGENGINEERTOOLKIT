How it works

Moisture content represents the amount of water contained in a material relative to its dry mass.

Example: If the wet mass is 110 kg and the dry mass is 100 kg:

(110 − 100) ÷ 100 × 100 = 10%

The wet mass should normally be greater than or equal to the dry mass.
