Introduction to Geology

Geology is the study of the Earth, including its materials, structure, processes and history. In mining engineering, geological knowledge helps engineers understand mineral deposits and the rocks surrounding them.

Minerals

💎 What is a Mineral?

A mineral is a naturally occurring inorganic solid with a relatively definite chemical composition and an ordered internal atomic structure.

Rocks

🌋 Igneous Rocks

Igneous rocks form when molten rock, such as magma or lava, cools and solidifies.

🌊 Sedimentary Rocks

Sedimentary rocks form from the accumulation, compaction and cementation of sediments or from chemical and biological processes.

⛰️ Metamorphic Rocks

Metamorphic rocks form when existing rocks are changed by heat, pressure or chemically active fluids without completely melting.

Geological Structures

📐 Folds

Folds are bends or curves in rock layers produced mainly by deformation of the Earth's crust.

⚡ Faults

A fault is a fracture or zone of fractures in rock along which movement has occurred.

Importance in Mining

Geological information helps mining engineers locate mineral deposits, understand ore-body geometry, assess ground conditions and support mine planning and safe extraction.
