About Mining Engineer Toolkit

Mining Engineer Toolkit is a practical digital platform designed to support mining engineering students, learners and professionals with everyday calculations and essential technical reference material.

The platform brings together mining calculators, engineering formulas, mining terminology and study resources in one simple and accessible place.

Its purpose is to make commonly used mining engineering calculations and concepts easier to understand, practise and apply.

  • 📐 Mining formulas and basic calculations
  • 📚 Mining engineering study references
  • ⛏️ Mining methods and equipment information
  • 🪨 Geology and mineral processing concepts
  • 🧨 Rock blasting fundamentals
  • 📊 Mine planning concepts
  • 📖 Mining terminology and definitions

Built for Learning

Mining Engineer Toolkit is designed with students in mind. The goal is not only to provide answers, but also to help users understand the engineering principles behind the calculations and concepts they encounter during their studies.

Our Vision

To develop a useful and accessible digital toolkit that grows alongside the mining engineering community, bringing practical calculations, technical knowledge and learning resources together in one platform.
