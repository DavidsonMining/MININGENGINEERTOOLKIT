How it works

Mine life is an estimate of how long the mine can operate at a specified annual production rate.

Example: 10,000,000 tonnes of mineable reserves produced at 500,000 tonnes per year gives:

10,000,000 ÷ 500,000 = 20 years

Actual mine life may vary because of production changes, recovery, dilution, economic factors and other mine planning considerations.
