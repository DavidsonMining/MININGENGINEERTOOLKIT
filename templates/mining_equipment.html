Introduction

Mining equipment consists of machines and tools used for activities such as drilling, excavation, loading, hauling, crushing and material handling. Selecting suitable equipment is important for safe and efficient mining operations.

Drilling Equipment

🛠️ Drill Rigs

Drill rigs are used to create holes in rock for exploration, blasting and other mining activities.

Loading Equipment

🚜 Excavators

Excavators are used to dig, load and move broken rock, ore and overburden. They are commonly used in both surface mining and construction operations.

🪣 Loaders

Loaders are mobile machines used to scoop and load material into haulage equipment or move material over short distances.

Haulage Equipment

🚛 Haul Trucks

Haul trucks transport ore, waste rock and other materials between loading areas, dumps, stockpiles and processing facilities.

Material Handling Equipment

🏗️ Conveyors

Conveyors transport bulk materials continuously between different locations within a mining or processing operation.

Equipment Selection Factors

Equipment selection can depend on factors such as material characteristics, production requirements, operating conditions, equipment capacity, haul distance, availability, safety and operating cost.
