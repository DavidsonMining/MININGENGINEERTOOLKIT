Common Mining Terms

Ore

A naturally occurring material containing valuable minerals that can be economically extracted.

Overburden

The soil and rock that lie above an ore deposit and must be removed to access the ore.

Stripping Ratio

The ratio of waste or overburden removed to the amount of ore extracted.

Bench

A step-like level created in an open-pit mine during excavation.

Haulage

The transportation of mined material from one location to another, usually using trucks or other haulage equipment.

Recovery

The proportion of valuable mineral successfully recovered from the material being processed.
