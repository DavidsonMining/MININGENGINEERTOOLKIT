How it works

The calculator estimates the volume of a rectangular pit or bench by multiplying its length, width and vertical height or depth.

Example: 100 m × 50 m × 10 m = 50,000 m³

This is an approximate calculation. Actual mine pit volumes may require bench geometry, slopes and other design parameters.
