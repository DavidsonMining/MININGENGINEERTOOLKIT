How it works

Recovery is the percentage of valuable metal successfully recovered from the amount of metal available in the ore.

Example: If 85 kg of metal is recovered from 100 kg available:

85 ÷ 100 × 100 = 85%

Recovery values are normally expressed as a percentage.
