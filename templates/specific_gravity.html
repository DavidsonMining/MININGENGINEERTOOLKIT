How it works

Specific gravity is the ratio of the density of a material to the density of water.

Example: If a material has a density of 2,700 kg/m³ and water has a density of 1,000 kg/m³:

2,700 ÷ 1,000 = 2.7

Specific gravity has no unit because it is a ratio.
